Network status indices, from the Foreground patch sizes:
  ECA      = sqrt(sum of squared patch sizes): size of one equally connected patch
  COH      = ECA / Foreground * 100: how coherent (connected) the network is
  REST_POT = 100 - COH: potential gain by connecting the patches
  RAC      = Foreground / (Foreground + Background) * 100
  CNOA     = minimum size of a new patch that would increase COH
